Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Estudio is a desktop application used only by its owner to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel through the YouTube API Services.

Data the app accesses

When the owner connects the app, Google grants it two permissions:

  • youtube.upload, to upload the owner's videos and set when they are published.
  • youtube.readonly, to read the connected channel's name, ID, subscriber count and video count, which the app shows on screen.

The app does not access any other data from the Google account and does not access data of any other YouTube user.

Data the app stores

Everything Estudio keeps is stored only on the owner's computer: the tokens Google issues, the connected channel's name, ID, subscriber count and video count (updated each time the app opens), and the ID, link and scheduled time of each video it uploaded. None of this data is sent to any server other than Google's.

Sharing

Estudio does not sell, share or transfer data received from Google APIs to anyone. To make each video, the app also uses other tools: Claude, by Anthropic, helps write the scripts, and ElevenLabs reads the narration. They never receive data from Google or YouTube.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Estudio's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube and Google

Estudio uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. The Google Privacy Policy also applies.

Revoking access and deleting data

You can revoke Estudio's access at any time from your Google Account security settings at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app's data folder on the computer removes all the information it stored.
