Estudio

A private Windows desktop app that its owner uses to make short videos and publish them on the owner's own YouTube channel.

What it does

  • Helps write, narrate and edit short vertical videos (YouTube Shorts) about video game news, in Spanish.
  • Uploads each finished video to the owner's own YouTube channel and schedules its publication time.
  • Marks every upload as containing altered or synthetic content, because the images and narration are generated with AI tools.

How it uses Google data

When the owner connects the app, Google asks for two permissions, and Estudio uses them only for this:

  • Manage your YouTube videos (youtube.upload): to upload the owner's own videos and set when they are published.
  • View your YouTube account (youtube.readonly): to show the connected channel's name inside the app.

Estudio does not access other users' data, does not sell or share any data, and keeps the access token only on the owner's computer. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Who can use it. Estudio is a personal tool. It is not distributed, sold or offered to other people, and only its owner signs in to it.
